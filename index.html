To my dearest you,

I've started this letter more times than I can count, because nothing I write ever feels big enough for what I feel when I'm with you.

Somewhere between our long talks, the way you laugh at my terrible jokes, and the quiet moments where nothing needs to be said, you became my favorite part of every day.

You make ordinary days feel worth remembering. You make me want to be better, kinder, braver. And every time I see you, I'm reminded how lucky I am that you're in my life.

So I gathered some flowers, put my whole heart on this page, and I'm finally asking you the question I've been holding onto for so long.

Will you be my girlfriend?
Always yours, Me

You said yes.

Thank you for choosing me. Here's to every page we'll write after this one.
